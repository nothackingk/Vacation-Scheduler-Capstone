Vacation Scheduler

An Android app for planning vacations and the excursions inside them. It includes a login, a date range report, and reminders for important dates.

Download the app (APK)

Version 1.0. Requires Android 8.0 or higher.

How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download the app.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps from this source.
  4. Tap Install, then open VacationScheduler.
  5. Tap Create an account to register, then log in.
